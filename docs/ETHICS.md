# Ethics and research data

## Ethics approval

The research data used in the study supporting the development and evaluation of HCC-PI were obtained following prior approval by the Comité de Ética para la Investigación en Seres Humanos del Hospital General Docente de Calderón (CEISH-HGDC-2024-007; August 18, 2024).

No individual-level research dataset is included in HCC-PI Monitor v1.0.0 or distributed through this software release.

## Scope

The approval above applies to the research data used in the supporting study. It does not represent clinical validation, regulatory approval, institutional endorsement of the software, or authorization to use HCC-PI Monitor for individual clinical decisions.

The public application contains demonstrative data. Contributors must not commit, upload, distribute, or enter identifiable patient information or an individual-level research dataset in this repository or its public demonstration.
